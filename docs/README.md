# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
This collection documents how OctoAcme initiates, plans, executes, releases, and improves cross-functional projects. It provides a shared, searchable source of truth for project managers, product managers, developers, QA, stakeholders, and sponsors.

## Project Management Process Summary
OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative delivery model with clear ownership and data-informed decisions. The process begins with initiation, when the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines measurable success criteria, and decides whether to move forward. During planning, approved work is broken into shippable increments, prioritized in a backlog, estimated, and mapped to milestones, dependencies, risks, and a Definition of Done. Execution is managed through a project board, regular standups, delivery syncs, reviews, testing, quality controls, and blocker escalation. Releases follow defined pre-release checks, deployment verification, communication, and rollback or incident procedures. After sprints, releases, or incidents, retrospectives capture lessons learned and convert them into tracked continuous-improvement actions. Risk management and stakeholder communication apply throughout the lifecycle, supported by defined roles and reusable templates.
