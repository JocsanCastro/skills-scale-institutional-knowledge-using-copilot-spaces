# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
This collection documents how OctoAcme initiates, plans, executes, releases, and improves cross-functional projects. It is intended to provide a shared, searchable source of truth for project managers, product managers, developers, QA, stakeholders, and sponsors.

## Project Management Process Summary
OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative delivery lifecycle with clear ownership and data-informed decisions. The process begins with initiation, where the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines measurable success criteria, and makes a go/no-go decision. During planning, approved work is broken into shippable increments, prioritized in a backlog, estimated, and mapped to milestones, dependencies, risks, and a Definition of Done. Execution is managed through a project board, regular standups, delivery syncs, reviews, testing, quality controls, and blocker escalation. Releases follow defined pre-release checks, deployment verification, communication, and rollback or incident procedures. After sprints, releases, or incidents, retrospectives capture learnings and convert them into tracked continuous-improvement actions. Risk management and stakeholder communication apply throughout the lifecycle, supported by defined roles and reusable templates.
